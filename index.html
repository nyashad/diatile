A little about me

Where science meets service.

I believe education should do more than share facts. It should awaken curiosity, build confidence and give every learner the tools to shape their future.

My work spans the classroom, STEM programmes, community leadership and writing. Across each space, the purpose is the same: to help people learn, participate and grow.
